How do I make one?
  1. Install a free scanning app: Polycam or KIRI Engine (iPhone or Android).
  2. Put the item on a plain table with good light and walk slowly around it while the app scans (photo mode works best).
  3. Crop away the table in the app, then export/share as GLB.
  4. Tap “Upload .glb file” here and pick it.
